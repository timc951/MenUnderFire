# Men Under Fire - Marketing & Pricing Documentation

This folder contains the marketing strategy and pricing analysis for Men Under Fire.

## Overview

**Men Under Fire** is a faith-based accountability community platform for men focused on:
- Weekly accountability check-ins
- Private groups for brotherhood
- Faith, fitness, and discipline tracking
- Spiritual strength and encouragement

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Christian men seeking accountability for:
- Faith/spiritual disciplines (prayer, Bible reading)
- Physical fitness
- Personal discipline and habit formation
- Brotherhood and community

**Secondary:** Churches and men's ministries looking for tools to support their men.

## Competitive Landscape

Similar platforms in the space:
- Covenant Eyes (accountability software, ~$16/mo)
- Fortify (recovery-focused, subscription)
- Hallow (Catholic prayer app, ~$9/mo)
- Pray.com (prayer community, ~$10/mo)
- Planning Center (church management, $0-400/mo)

## Recommended Strategy

**Hybrid: Organization-Pays + Free Members**

1. Churches and ministries pay for premium features
2. Individual members use the platform for free
3. Optional donation support for independent users
4. Focus on 10-50 paying organizations for sustainability
