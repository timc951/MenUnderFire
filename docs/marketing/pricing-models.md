# Pricing Models Analysis

## Overview

This document analyzes three potential pricing models for Men Under Fire.

---

## Option 1: Donation-Based

### Viability: Possible but challenging

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Aligns with ministry/faith mission | Unpredictable revenue |
| Low barrier to entry = maximum reach | Typical conversion: 1-3% donate |
| Builds goodwill and trust | Hard to cover costs as you scale |
| Tax benefits if registered as 501(c)(3) | Requires constant fundraising |

### When Donation-Based Works

- Strong existing community/church backing
- Low infrastructure costs
- Volunteer leadership
- Regular newsletter/update cadence to remind supporters

### Requirements for Success

To make donations work, you'd need:
- ~50-100 committed supporters giving $5-10/month, OR
- 2-3 "anchor donors" covering infrastructure
- Monthly impact updates (testimonies, stats)
- A Patreon/Ko-fi/Buy Me a Coffee or direct Stripe donations page
- Churches/men's ministries willing to sponsor

---

## Option 2: Freemium Model

### Viability: Strong and sustainable

| Tier | Price | Features |
|------|-------|----------|
| **Free** | $0 | Join 1 group, weekly check-ins, basic accountability |
| **Forged** | $7/month | Unlimited groups, progress history, badges, direct messaging |
| **Brotherhood** | $15/month | All above + group leadership tools, custom prompts, priority support |

### Why This Works

- Free tier drives adoption and word-of-mouth
- Price points are accessible (less than a coffee/week)
- Faith audience often willing to pay for tools that support spiritual growth
- Comparable to existing apps: Hallow ($9/mo), Pray.com ($10/mo), Covenant Eyes ($16/mo)

### Revenue Projections

| Active Users | 5% Conversion | At $7/mo Avg |
|--------------|---------------|--------------|
| 500 | 25 paying | $175/month |
| 1,000 | 50 paying | $350/month |
| 5,000 | 250 paying | $1,750/month |

---

## Option 3: Hybrid (Donation + Optional Premium)

### Structure

- Core platform: 100% free, donation-supported
- Optional premium features for those who want to contribute more
- Transparent "help keep the lights on" messaging

### Implementation

1. Free access to all core features
2. "Support the Mission" donation button prominently displayed
3. Optional premium tier with bonus features
4. Monthly transparency updates showing funding status

This model aligns with ministry values while providing sustainable revenue.

---

## Option 4: Organization-Pays (Recommended)

See [Organization Model](./organization-model.md) for full details.

| Who | Pays? | Gets |
|-----|-------|------|
| Churches / Men's Ministries | Yes | Admin tools, branding, analytics |
| Individual Members | No | Full access to groups, check-ins |

---

## Recommendation

**Start with Organization-Pays + Donation Hybrid:**

1. Launch free for individuals with donation option
2. Actively sell to churches/ministries at $79-149/month
3. Focus on getting 10-20 paying organizations
4. Add individual premium tier later based on user feedback

This approach:
- Maximizes adoption (free for members)
- Provides predictable B2B revenue
- Aligns with church budget cycles and buying patterns
- Reduces pressure on individual donations
